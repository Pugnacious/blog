# Mole
This is a small guide on various builds for the Mole in Star Citizen.

## Prep Work
The first step is to prep.

Ok, That's super corny, but not entirely inaccurate.

Grab yourself an 8-SCU box and put it on your Mole's cargo grid. You Want to fill it with the following.

  - Sabirs at a minimum (as many as you think you might need)
  - Optional: Okunis/Boremax/Stalwart/Waveshift. These can make life easier
  - You should also have several Focus III, Torrent III, Surge, Stampede, Reiger C3 modules available to swap out as needed.

## Builds
### Big Breaker (High mass, high resistance (Savrilium and Gold))

Here's the three mining head's and the recommended modules.

  - Helix 2 
    * Deluge
    * Deluge
    * Focus III
  - Hofstede 2
    * Deluge
    * Focus III
  - Hofstede 2
    * Deluge
    * Focus III

#### Note:
Load a Helix 2 on Cargo Grid for larger rocks, that way you can swap it out as needed.


### Medium (Medium mass, medium-high resistance (Stileron and friends) 
A Sabir and the Helix should be able to pop any of the Stileron rocks in space if you are by yourself. The Helix and Hofstede together can break any of the stileron rocks you find. The Arbor 
  - Helix 2 
    * Deluge
    * Deluge
    * Focus III
  - Hofstede 2
    * Deluge
    * Focus III
  - Arbor MH2 (This laser is for the little chunks that the Hofstede will overcharge)
    * Focus I
    * Focus I

### The Fragiles (Ouratite, Beryl, the like)
Some of these rocks are going to require careful positioning, smaller ouratite nodes will need you to be on the low power laser and close to max range to not shatter. Moving modules around may also be required, such as removing a focus I and replacing it with a deluge for some of the ouratite rocks that would otherwise be 100% resistance)
